Campaign Message

A message to every health student

Fellow health students,

Each of us chose this path to serve. Yet too often our voices are scattered across programmes, campuses and regions, and our concerns go unheard when they matter most. I am asking for your vote to change that.

I have worked in clinical care, studied public health and epidemiology, and am pursuing medicine and law. That means I have seen from several sides what holds health professionals back, and what it takes to move us forward.

As your President, I will lead with competence, transparency and one clear commitment: one voice for every student, one health standard for every community, and one future for the next generation of Ghana’s health workforce.

United by Purpose. Driven by Excellence. Vote Commey Nii Sheriff, #3.

My Agenda

One Voice. One Health. One Future.

#OneVoice

A united, transparent association.

  • Regular town halls open to every programme and campus
  • Open reporting on decisions and finances
  • Advocacy for student welfare, backed by legal insight

#OneHealth

Excellence in training and in service.

  • Mentorship linking students with practising professionals
  • Community outreach rooted in public health practice
  • Evidence-led health education campaigns

#OneFuture

Preparing graduates to lead.

  • Guidance on licensing and career pathways
  • Awareness of labour rights for young health workers
  • Partnerships that open doors for students
